# Meniscus

A KDE Plasma desktop that stays out of your way. Everything opens from a shortcut, a hot corner or a quick menu, and only what matters right now is on screen. Its surfaces are Liquid Glass that pours out of the screen's edges and back in, tinted to one of twelve themes.

**Meniscus Flow** is the same workflow without the look: it keeps your current Plasma theme.

Work in progress: there is no installer yet.
